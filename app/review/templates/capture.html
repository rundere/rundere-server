Bookmarklet — one click on any booking page

Drag this to your bookmarks bar. When you’re looking at a confirmation page (even one behind a login), click it — Yonder captures the page you’re viewing and queues it. This is the reliable path: your browser has already loaded and authenticated the page.

If a page blocks the direct send (some heavy or strict-CSP sites do), Yonder copies the page’s HTML to your clipboard instead — just paste it into the box on the right.

{# --- Fallback path: paste source or a public URL --- #}

Or paste something — pick what it is

Choose what you’re capturing, then paste it. Directions links need a Travel date and a travel mode (a route carries neither). Login-walled pages can’t be fetched from a URL — use the bookmarklet or paste the source for those. Travellers come from the trip/collection you attach it to, not this form.

Date/time are only used to fill gaps the page itself doesn’t state (e.g. an event with no listed time) — a date or time on the page always wins, and you can also just fix it later in the review queue.
