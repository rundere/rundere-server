About Run.Dere

{# Dictionary-style name card: say it, hear it, know what it was called before. #}
Run.Dere / run-THAIR /
noun · said like “run there”

Your trips, from the booking email to the day you go — captured, planned and ready, so all that's left is to run there. Formerly Yonder; renamed in 4.4.0.

Run.Dere turns travel documents — booking emails, calendar invites, PDF confirmations, Google Maps directions, and web pages — into clean records in your self-hosted AdventureLog (trips & places) and AirTrail (flights). You capture, it extracts and resolves, you review, and it logs — nothing is written without your approval.
