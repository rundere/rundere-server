About Yonder

Yonder turns travel documents — booking emails, calendar invites, PDF confirmations, Google Maps directions, and web pages — into clean records in your self-hosted AdventureLog (trips & places) and AirTrail (flights). You capture, it extracts and resolves, you review, and it logs — nothing is written without your approval.
