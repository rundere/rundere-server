{# What it is, Run·dere-first (D-146): AdventureLog and AirTrail are optional extras. #}

Run·dere is a self-hosted travel planner. Forward a booking email, paste a link, or drop in a PDF or calendar file: Run·dere reads it, you approve what it found, and it lands on your trip's timeline and map. It also researches the places you're going, plans what to pack, tracks and splits costs with the people you travel with, and keeps a travel journal. There's an iPhone and iPad app, and AI assistants such as Claude can work with your trips when you let them.

This server

{% if demo %}

This is the Run·dere demo server. It holds sample trips for one fictional traveller: one under way, a road trip coming up and one from earlier in the year. Look around the timeline, map, Discover research, packing, costs and journal. The demo is read-only: you can see everything, but nothing can be added or changed, and the sample trips' dates are moved forward each night so they stay current. {% if not signed_in %}The sign-in details are on the sign-in page.{% endif %}

To capture your own bookings, run your own Run·dere server. See rundere.app, and how the demo handles your data.

{% else %}

This Run·dere server is run by whoever set it up. The trips, bookings and journal on it live here, on this server: they aren't sent to the Run·dere developer.
